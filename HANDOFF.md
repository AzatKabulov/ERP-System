## 3. Decisions recorded (see the table in PLAN.md)

D1 Django + DRF + PostgreSQL (confirmed). D3 business currency TMT; selling price may be stated in TMT or USD, converted with an owner/manager-entered rate that is stored as history; costs, payments, totals and reports are TMT only. D6 costing: each purchase keeps its own cost and the oldest batch is used first (FIFO). D8a warranty terms per product (months, 0 = none, plus text). D9 first scanning method: tablet camera. D16 password recovery by emailed one-time code. **Owner answers on 2026-10-07 (Phases 7-8):** the return period is per product, typed by hand in days and counted from the day of the sale; the warranty stays in months (a separate field); any seller may refund money; an expired or missing warranty can be accepted only by an owner or manager (with a note); the photo of an expense receipt is optional. **Owner answers on 2026-10-07 (earlier):** prices are not fixed (the seller sets any price); no tax, no invoices, no legal receipt format, keep one simple receipt; this is an ERP, not a cash register (no change, no amounts tendered, no split payments: only cash or card as a label); selling is in-store and online only; customers stay optional; keep everything simple (Turkmenistan: little internet, old-fashioned). D5 permission matrix, D4 (no tax, `S-000001` numbering) and D10 default language (Russian) are **provisional**.

**Assumptions made without the owner while building Phases 1-4** (all easy to change now, costly later; to confirm):

- Permission matrix: only the owner manages business settings, locations and staff; the manager manages catalog, suppliers, purchase orders, exchange rates, opening stock and adjustments; the warehouse receives goods but sees no costs; sales sees stock quantities at their own locations but no history or costs.
- FIFO means the oldest batch is used first (not average cost). USD applies to selling prices only. The seller types the price of every sale line, so the exchange rate only matters for the catalog's starting suggestion (conversion at sale was dropped on 2026-10-07).
- Opening stock needs a unit cost and is allowed once per product and location; corrections are adjustments with a mandatory reason, posted by owner or manager (D7 open).
- Receiving more than is outstanding is refused. The receipt cost is the cost on the order line. Cancelling a partly received order keeps what arrived.
- Staff are new accounts only; email is required and unique; password at least 10 characters; access token 15 minutes, refresh token 14 days; a reused refresh token just returns 401.
- Purchase orders are numbered `PO-0001...` per business.
- All new Turkmen text, the Turkmen document labels and the default Turkmen unit names are drafts for a fluent speaker to review.

**Assumptions made without the owner while building Phase 5** (to confirm):

- Sale numbers are `S-000001...` per business, taken last in the transaction so there are no gaps; they are only a plain reference.
- Any seller may charge any price, including zero or below cost; the catalog price is only the starting suggestion and stays on the line for reference. The owner sees a loss in the history.
- Payment is only a label, Cash (default) or Card; bank transfer was dropped. No debt or credit sales.
- A completed sale cannot be edited or deleted. Returns and corrections are Phase 7.
- Cost and profit are visible to owner and manager only; they are never printed on the receipt.
- The cart does not reserve stock: if another tablet sells the goods first, the server refuses with the product name and the cart is kept.
- If the answer to "Complete sale" is lost, the cart is emptied and the saved record owns that sale (the pending banner shows it) so the same goods are not sold twice by hand.
- The receipt is one plain 80 mm page of my own design; its language is the business's document-language setting; address and phone (Settings) are printed when filled in.
- Selling needs the server (online only); the restart-safe resend covers a flaky connection. No offline mode.

**Assumptions made without the owner while building Phase 6** (to confirm):

- A transfer is dispatched the moment it is sent (no separate draft); there is one receipt per transfer, and anything that did not arrive is written off with a reason (no repeated partial receipts). The sender's side cancels; the destination cannot.
- During a stock count sales and receipts continue (nothing is frozen, D13). The count adjusts by what the counter found against what the system showed when the count started, on top of the current stock; lines that moved meanwhile are flagged. If goods were sold meanwhile and the decrease no longer fits, the approval is refused and the count must be recounted or cancelled.
- Only owner and manager approve a count (D7). Counters see the system quantity while counting (no blind count). Goods found that the system did not know about are costed at the latest cost layer here (else anywhere, else the product's default cost, else zero).
- Warehouse staff can send, receive and count at their own locations; sales staff have no transfers or counts.

**Assumptions made without the owner while building Phases 7 and 8** (to confirm):

- A return goes back to the location of the sale, and the refund is exactly the price charged x quantity (returning the whole rest of a line refunds what is left of its total, so no cents go missing). The money goes back by the sale's own label (cash or card); this is an ERP, there is no till. The refund cannot be typed by hand.
- The return period binds everybody, owner and manager included. A product with no period has none (no limit); 0 means it is not taken back. Old sale lines (from before the field existed) have no limit.
- An exchange is a return plus a normal new sale (no special link). A return that is "awaiting inspection" is decided later by an owner or manager (sellable or damaged).
- A supplier return takes the oldest cost layers (FIFO) and credits the delivery's price (shown only with cost access). It can only return what that delivery brought, less what was already sent back.
- The reorder list suggests `target - (on hand + on order)` for a product below its minimum at a place; open orders count, drafts do not. Minimums are set per product and place on the product page.
- Default expense categories: Аренда, Зарплата, Коммунальные услуги, Транспорт, Прочее (owner and manager can add more in the app; the default names are Russian even for a Turkmen business). An expense can be corrected (the old and new values go to the audit trail) or voided with a reason, never deleted.
- Receipt files: JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF up to 5 MB, kept in a private folder on the server and served only through the API to a signed-in member with the right.
- Warranty: counted in months from the day of the sale, the last day included. A warranty refund is an ordinary customer return into `damaged` (the return window does not apply). A replacement gives out one sellable piece and takes one damaged piece back at the line's average cost. A claim cannot be larger than what was sold and not yet returned.
- CSV import only creates **new** products (an existing SKU is a row error), at most 2000 rows and 1 MB, `;` or `,` as delimiter, decimal comma accepted; the unit is given by name or symbol; categories and brands are created when named; stock is never touched. Export is UTF-8 with a BOM and `;`, with the cost column only for roles that see costs. (A very large import was measured at about 16 s for 2000 rows locally.)
- Every new Turkmen string is a draft for a fluent speaker to review.

**Assumptions made without the owner while building Phase 9** (to confirm; the owner left the choice of reports to me, keeping it simple):

- **Reports offered:** summary, sales, stock (below the minimum, stock value, movements), purchasing, returns, expenses, and the activity history; each with a CSV export. If the owner wants a different set or other columns, say so: the CSV columns are my design (`date;sales_count;revenue;refunds;net_sales` for sales and similar for the others).
- **Definitions:** revenue = the sales made in the period; refunds are counted by the day of the return, whenever the sale was; net sales = revenue - refunds; cost of goods = the FIFO cost of the period's sales minus the cost carried back by the period's returns (damaged or awaiting-inspection returns included: they carry their cost back too); gross profit = net sales - cost of goods; the result = gross profit - expenses (an operating result, **not** an accounting profit; no tax, no wages beyond what is entered as an expense).
- **Stock value** counts every condition (sellable, damaged, awaiting inspection, in transit) at the FIFO cost of the remaining layers, split by condition on screen. Items below the minimum are the products with a saved minimum whose sellable stock is lower.
- **Purchasing:** orders created in the period are all counted (with their statuses), but only orders actually placed (ordered, partly received, received) count as money ordered; drafts and cancelled orders do not. Deliveries are dated by their receipt.
- **Periods:** today, the last 7 days, this month and last month, from the tablet's date; the server reads the days in the business's time zone, at most 366 days. A place can be chosen; owners and managers see all places.
- **Who sees what:** only owner and manager open the Reports page and the activity history. The dashboard goes to everybody but with only their sections: a seller sees today's and the month's sales at their own places and no costs; a keeper sees stock and orders and no money.
- Action names in the activity history are translated (Turkmen is a draft); an action the app has no name for is shown by its code.

**Receiving by scanning (owner request, 2026-10-10). Assumptions made without the owner, to confirm:**

- **Cost is optional, as the owner said.** Only owner and manager can enter one. A line without a cost uses the product's default purchase cost, else **zero**, and is stored with `cost_known = false`. Reports do not know that flag yet, so profit on goods received without a cost is **overstated** until a cost is entered, and there is no screen to fill a missing cost in afterwards (PLAN 9.8: needs a decision on how a correction should work).
- **Who may receive:** owner, manager and warehouse keeper (`intake.view`, `intake.create`); sellers may not. Easy to change in `businesses/permissions.py`.
- **A new product from an unknown code** gets the code as its article number (and as its barcode, unless it is a long text or a web address, then the article is typed), the first whole-pieces unit unless another is chosen, price 0 (prices are typed at the sale), no category or brand. The owner may want to tidy these in the catalog afterwards.
- **Matching:** a code is looked up as a barcode first and then as an article number (any letter case). This is also what the sales screen uses now, and Enter in the sales search adds an exact code straight to the cart (a name is still an ordinary search).
- **Camera counting rule:** an item is counted once per "visit": the same code is counted again only after nothing was read for 0.7 s; when a label carries a barcode and a QR code the barcode wins. This is a guess about how fast items are swapped; it needs a real tablet (item 14 below). Two codes that resolve to one product merge into one line.
- **No internet lookup of product data** (the owner's idea). Reasons are in ARCHITECTURE ("Receiving by scanning") and PLAN 9.7: the boxes carry manufacturer part numbers, which free barcode databases do not know; car-parts catalogues are paid; nothing could be tested from here; it would send scans to a third party. Cheaper alternatives are listed in PLAN 9.7 (import the supplier's price list; read the label text with the camera).
- The unfinished list lives only on the device (one per person and business), by design: it is a draft, not a document, until it is sent.

## 5. Environment notes (Claude Code cloud)

- Fresh VM: `bash scripts/setup_claude_cloud.sh`, then `source scripts/claude_cloud_env.sh` in each shell. Start the local database with `bash scripts/claude_cloud_postgres.sh` (writes the git-ignored `backend/.env`), then run the backend commands from `backend/` as listed in AGENTS.md.
- `dl.google.com` is blocked in this environment, so there is no local Android SDK. GitHub Actions builds the APK instead. This is an environment limit, not an application defect.
- Reachable: `github.com`, `pub.dev`, `storage.googleapis.com`, the Python package index, `maven.google.com`, `services.gradle.org`.
- Git: ordinary pushes to the assigned branch only, never forced.
- Tests that drive taps through `tapKey` (`mobile/test/support/real_rig.dart`) pump frame by frame after scrolling (a scrollable ignores taps until its scroll animation has finished) and once after the tap. Use `goBack` to leave a pushed screen: the route needs about 800 ms to leave.
- The real-stack browser run is in `scripts/e2e/` (README there). It reads receipts back with `scripts/e2e/pdf_text.py` (pypdf, a backend dev dependency). Do not run `reset_db.sh` against anything but a throwaway local database: it flushes it.
- Avoid `pkill -f` patterns that also match the command line of your own shell; use a bracket trick in a separate command or look up the PID first.
